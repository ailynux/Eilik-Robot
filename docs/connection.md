## Troubleshooting

*This section will be expanded as we learn more about the connection process.*

### Common Issues

- **Device not found**: Make sure your Eilik is powered on and in pairing mode
- **Connection timeout**: Try moving the robot closer to your computer
- **Permission errors**: Ensure Bluetooth permissions are granted on your system
